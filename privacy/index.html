This Privacy Policy applies to all apps we publish on the App Store (“our apps”, “we”, “us”). It explains what information our apps handle, why, who else is involved, and the choices you have. Not every app uses every feature described here: for example, only some apps show ads, use AI analysis or connect to Apple Health. Each section applies only to apps that have the feature it describes.

01Information We Collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number in our apps.

Data you enter in our apps (such as goals, body measurements, logs, history and settings) is stored locally on your device.

Our apps may also collect, automatically:

  • Subscription data: an anonymous identifier, purchase information and basic technical information (such as device model, operating system version and app version), used to manage your subscription;
  • Analytics and crash data, described in section 3;
  • Advertising data, described in section 4, in apps that show ads.

We do not sell your personal data.

02Features That Use a Server

Some features (for example AI analysis of a photo or a text description) need to send data to a server to work. In that case:

  • only the data needed for that feature is sent, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS);
  • the content you send is used only to return a result to you and is not stored after the request is completed;
  • it is not used to identify you, for advertising, or to train AI models, and it is never sold.

Hosting providers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for a short period for security and reliability.

03Analytics and Crash Reports

To understand how our apps are used and to fix errors, our apps may use analytics and crash reporting services. They automatically collect:

  • Usage data, such as which screens are opened, which features are used, app launches and session length;
  • Crash and diagnostic data, such as crash logs, error details and app performance;
  • Technical data, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate country or region (derived from your IP address);
  • an app-specific installation identifier.

This data is not linked to your name or contact details and does not include the content you enter (such as photos, logs or health data).

04Advertising

Some of our apps, or their free versions, show ads provided by an advertising network. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the advertising network may collect:

  • Device and technical data, such as device model, operating system version, language, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped;
  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow it.

Apps that show ads ask for your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used. If you do not allow tracking, you will see non-personalized ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we also ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

You can change your choice at any time: in the iOS Settings app (Privacy & Security › Tracking), or through the privacy or ad settings option in the app, where available. Ads are never based on your health data, photos or the content you enter.

05Service Providers

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We use a small number of trusted service providers to run our apps:

  • payment and subscription management (all payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details);
  • app analytics and crash reporting;
  • advertising (in apps that show ads);
  • server hosting;
  • AI processing for features that analyze photos or text;
  • public product databases (for example, only a barcode number is sent when you scan a barcode).

These providers receive only the data needed to perform their service and are required to protect it at a level at least equal to this Privacy Policy. The advertising network also processes data under its own privacy policy.

06Apple Health

If you give permission, some of our apps read from or write to Apple Health (for example nutrition, weight or steps). Health data stays on your device and in Apple Health. It is not sent to our servers, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising or marketing. You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app or the Health app.

07Camera and Photos

Apps that analyze photos ask for camera or photo library access. Photos are used only for the feature you started. Photos you save in an app are stored on your device. Photos are never shared with advertising or analytics services.

08Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not collect notification data.

09Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting it in the app or deleting the app. Content sent to a server for processing is not retained after the request is completed. Analytics and crash data is kept for a limited period (typically up to 14 months) and then deleted. Advertising data is kept by the advertising network according to its own policy.

10Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union under the GDPR, or in California under the CCPA), you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Since we do not keep personal data linked to your identity, most requests are fulfilled by deleting the app’s data on your device and changing your tracking or ad choices as described in section 4. For any other request, contact us at the email below.

11Disclosure Required by Law

We may disclose information if required by law, court order or a public authority, or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

12Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

13Data Security

Data on your device is protected by your device’s security (such as passcode, Face ID and encryption). Data sent to servers is encrypted in transit.

14Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The Effective Date at the top shows when it was last changed.
