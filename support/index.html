Frequently asked questions

Restoring a purchase

If you already bought a subscription and it is not active, open any Premium screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your device open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select the app and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Open your subscriptions

Refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders are not arriving

First check that notifications are allowed: open the iOS Settings app, find the app, then Notifications. Then confirm the reminder is switched on in the app’s settings.

Camera or Apple Health access

If you declined access earlier, open the iOS Settings app, find the app and turn on Camera or Photos.

For Apple Health, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, select the app and turn on the data you want to share.

AI results look wrong

Values calculated by AI (such as calories and nutrients) are estimates. For the best results, take the photo from above in good light with the whole plate visible.

You can edit any item, its portion and its values before saving, or add a short note to correct the result. For packaged food, scanning the barcode or the nutrition label gives exact values.
